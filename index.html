chris howard

i'm an associate professor of philosophy at mcgill university, where i direct the philosophy, technology, & policy lab.

most of my work to date has been in ethics & metaethics. i'm now working on questions about legitimacy & governance in digital infrastructure. my current project focuses on the concentration of power in centralized social media platforms & ai systems, & on the prospects for decentralized alternatives. i'm also thinking about the ethics & politics of ai alignment & personhood.

before joining mcgill, i was a research assistant professor at unc-chapel hill & core faculty in the unc/duke program in philosophy, politics, & economics. i completed my ph.d. at the university of arizona (2017) & an m.a. from brandeis (2011). i was a visiting fellow at harvard in 2017 & at the anu in 2019.

i co-organize CHillNorm with Alex Worsnip & Margaret Shea.

the best way to get in touch with me is by email at chris.howard@mcgill.ca. google scholar here.
